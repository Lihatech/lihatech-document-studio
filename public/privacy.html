Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how LihaTech Solutions handles information in LihaTech Learn & Document Studio at studio.lihatech.com. It covers registered students, authorized staff and the Google account connected for interview scheduling.

Information we handleGoogle accessSharingYour choices

1. Information we handle and why

  • Account information: Google sign-in supplies identity information such as your account identifier, email address and name. Firebase authenticates your account; the portal checks your registered email and role to control access. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Profile and batch information: we store your name, optional title, student ID, batch assignment and access dates. If you provide them, we also store your phone number, address, education or employment details to support your programme and requests.
  • Document information: we store certificate or offer-letter requests, proposed corrections and dates, internship roles, admin decisions and issued-document details so staff can review and provide the appropriate document.
  • Interview information: we store requested times, topics you have prepared, booking status and meeting details to arrange and manage mock interviews.
  • Administrative records: authorized staff may manage business contacts, invoices, letters, certificates and payroll-related records in Document Studio. These records are not made available to student accounts.
  • Service information: the portal stores notifications and read status. Authentication and hosting services may process connection, device and diagnostic information to operate and protect the service.

2. Google sign-in and Calendar access

For students: Google sign-in is used to identify your account. The current student sign-in flow does not request access to your Google Calendar, Gmail inbox or Google Drive files.

For the LihaTech organizer: the organizer separately authorizes Calendar access. The backend uses that authorization to read availability, read calendar event titles and times for authorized admins, and create, update or cancel mock-interview events with Google Meet details. Other calendar event titles and guest information are not exposed to students; students see availability and their own booking information.

When an interview is approved, the student's name, email, scheduled time and prepared topics may be included in the Google Calendar event and shared with the organizer and invited interviewer through Google. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information in prepared topics.

The organizer's OAuth refresh token is configured on the server so the integration can obtain access tokens. It is not provided to student browsers. The organizer can revoke authorization from their Google Account; this stops the Calendar integration until it is reconnected.

Information received from Google APIs is used to provide and support the portal's sign-in and scheduling features, not sold, used for advertising or used to train generalized AI models. LihaTech's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Who can receive information

Authorized LihaTech staff can access information needed to administer the service and review requests. Interview information is shared with the relevant organizer and interviewer. Documents contain the approved details and can be downloaded by the authorized recipient.

The portal uses Google/Firebase for authentication and Google Calendar/Meet for interviews, Supabase for database and private-file services, and hosting infrastructure to serve the website and API. These providers process information needed to deliver their services and operate under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. Information may be processed outside your country depending on provider locations and configuration.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to investigate misuse and protect the service. This portal does not include advertising trackers.

4. Storage, security and retention

The portal uses role-based access checks and authenticated services to restrict private information. No online service can guarantee complete security. Keep your Google account secure and avoid using shared devices for private documents without signing out afterward.

Account, request and administrative records are retained to operate the service, maintain issued-document history and resolve support or security issues. A batch ending, signing out, or revoking Google access does not automatically delete stored records. Contact us to request a retention review or deletion; records that need to be retained for legitimate administrative or legal reasons will be explained when handling the request. Provider logs and backups may follow separate retention schedules.

5. Browser storage and external links

The signed-in portal uses browser storage to maintain authentication, interface preferences and cached application information. Clearing browser storage may sign you out or reset preferences; it does not delete server-side records. The public About, Privacy and Terms pages do not require sign-in or run analytics scripts.

Links to Google Meet, Google Drive or other websites are governed by those services' policies. Opening a third-party link can share connection information with that service.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You can update the editable details in your profile and choose not to supply optional fields. Contact LihaTech to correct your registered email or batch assignment, ask for access to your information, or request deletion. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Revoking Google authorization does not itself remove existing portal records.

For privacy questions or requests, email lihatech7070@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify your registered account, but never send your password, sign-in token or private credentials.

7. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when the portal's features or data practices change. The latest version and update date will appear on this page. New uses of Google data that require additional permission will require the relevant authorization.
