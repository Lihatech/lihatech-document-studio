How access works

Students sign in with the Google account whose email LihaTech has registered. Signing in alone does not grant access: your registration and assigned role determine what you can see. Administrative tools are restricted to approved staff.

Student sign-in does not request access to students' Google Calendars. LihaTech connects its organizer account separately to check availability, display calendar events to authorized admins, and create or cancel interview invitations.
